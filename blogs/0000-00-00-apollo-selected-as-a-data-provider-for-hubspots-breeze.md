Apollo's 230M+ contact database and 30M+ company data is now embedded in HubSpot's Breeze Prospecting Agent, available in public beta with a 28-day free trial, letting HubSpot users with Apollo accounts source contacts and run outreach without leaving HubSpot. Apollo cites an average 97% email accuracy from its multi-step verification process.
