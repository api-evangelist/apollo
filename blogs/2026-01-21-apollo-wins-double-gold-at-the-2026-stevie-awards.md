Apollo won two Gold Stevie Awards at the 20th Annual Stevie Awards for Sales & Customer Service: AI-Powered Sales or Service Solution for its Agentic End-to-End GTM Platform, and Product Feature of the Year for its AI Assistant. It marks Apollo's second consecutive year of Gold recognition, with judges calling the unified platform that replaces 6-7 separate tools "category-defining."
